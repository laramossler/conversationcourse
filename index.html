The LinkedIn messages that feel like form letters. The networking events where everyone is performing. The dinners where conversation dies after "what do you do?"

Questions You Might Have

Is this therapy or coaching?

Neither. This is skill development. We're not processing trauma or setting life goals—we're learning and practicing specific techniques that make you better at conversation. Think of it more like a language class than a therapy session.

Do I need to share personal things?

You control your boundaries completely. Practice scenarios can be professional, hypothetical, or personal—your choice. The skills transfer regardless of the content you practice with.

What if I miss a session?

All sessions are recorded and available to cohort members. That said, the live participation is where transformation happens—you can't practice presence in a recording. Plan to attend live when possible.

Will this make me manipulative?

The opposite. Manipulation is about hiding your intentions to get what you want. Genuine conversation skill is about being more present, more curious, more real. People who try to use these techniques manipulatively quickly discover they don't work that way.

I'm introverted. Is this for me?

Absolutely. This isn't about becoming an extrovert or talking more. It's about making the conversations you do have more meaningful. Many introverts find that better conversation skills actually reduce the exhaustion of social interaction.

How is this different from communication training?

Most communication training focuses on presenting, public speaking, or negotiation tactics. This is specifically about one-on-one and small group conversation—the unstructured moments where real relationships form and real decisions happen.
