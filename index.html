Four Weeks to Confident Conversation

Week 1: Opening

Learn to start conversations that invite connection

  • Master presence and first impressions
  • Craft open-ended questions
  • Read the room before you speak

Week 2: Connecting

Build rapport through active listening

  • Move beyond small talk
  • Balance speaking and listening
  • Find common ground naturally

Week 3: Making Introductions

Introduce yourself and others with grace

  • The gracious self-introduction
  • Introducing others effectively
  • Handle forgotten names without awkwardness

Week 4: Practice & Polish

Put it all together in real scenarios

  • Live practice with feedback
  • Common challenging situations
  • Your personal conversation style

Format:

  • Short video lessons (10-15 min each, watch at your pace)
  • 4 live small-group practice sessions (1 per week, ~60 min)
  • Real-time feedback from instructor
  • Practice scenarios: meeting people at events, dinner conversations, networking
